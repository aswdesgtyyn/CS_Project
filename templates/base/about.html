About us

Who We Are

Indigo Smart is a next-generation cab service company dedicated to transforming urban mobility through innovation, reliability, and customer-centric solutions. Founded with a vision to make commuting safer, faster, and more intelligent, Indigo Smart is committed to providing world-class transportation experiences for everyone.



What We Do

We provide a range of on-demand and scheduled transportation services designed for individuals, families, and businesses. Whether you're commuting to work, traveling to the airport, or just heading across town, Indigo Smart ensures that you get there on time and in comfort.

  • On-demand cab bookings
  • Scheduled rides
  • Corporate transportation solutions
  • Safe and verified drivers


Our Mission

To redefine the cab experience by integrating smart technology, outstanding service, and a commitment to customer safety and satisfaction.
