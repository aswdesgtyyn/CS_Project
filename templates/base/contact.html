FAQ

1. What is Indigo Smart?

Indigo Smart is a tech-enabled cab service committed to providing safe, reliable, and comfortable transportation. We offer on-demand and scheduled rides across urban and semi-urban areas.



2. Is Indigo Smart available 24/7?

Yes, our services are available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.



3. How are fares calculated?

Fares are calculated based on:

  • Base fare
  • Distance traveled
  • Time taken
  • Applicable surge pricing during high-demand periods

A fare estimate is shown before you confirm your booking.



4. Can I schedule a ride in advance?

Absolutely. You can schedule a ride up to 7 days in advance using our app or website.



5. Is there a cancellation fee?

Yes, a minimal cancellation fee may apply if:

  • The cab has already been dispatched
  • The cancellation is made beyond the free cancellation window (usually 5–10 minutes after booking)
